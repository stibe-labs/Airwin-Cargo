Customer Care Executive

Airwin Cargo Services is seeking a qualified Customer Care Executive to join our team in Calicut Airport Jn.. Become part of a distinguished global logistics group operating across India, the UAE, and international trade gateways.

Job Location Calicut Airport Jn.
Salary (negotiable)
Job Type Full-time
Department Customer Support

Role Overview & Key Responsibilities

  • Act as the primary customer support liaison for global and domestic cargo inquiries.
  • Provide accurate airway bill tracking, scheduled delivery ETAs, and customs status updates to clients.
  • Coordinate with Calicut Airport junction logistics dispatchers and operations teams for express turnaround.
  • Maintain meticulous service logs, record client feedback, and escalate transit exceptions swiftly.
  • Uphold Airwin's 25-year reputation for mission-critical cargo customer satisfaction.

Qualifications & Requirements

  • Language proficiency: Fluent in Hindi and English (both written and spoken).
  • Basic Computer Knowledge, email correspondence, and ability to learn internal freight tracking portals.
  • Candidates may be Freshers or Experienced — training will be provided.
  • Excellent active listening, courteous phone manner, and client-centric problem solving skills.
  • Familiarity with air cargo status inquiries, waybills, and courier delivery tracking is an advantage.
